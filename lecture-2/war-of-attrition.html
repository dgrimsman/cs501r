War of Attrition

Two players fight over a prize worth v > 1. Each stage costs 1 (discounted by δ) to keep fighting. Whoever concedes first (plays Q) hands the prize to the other player; if both concede on the same stage, no one gets it. Choose a deterministic concession stage for each player, or a per-stage concession probability, and watch how it plays out.
