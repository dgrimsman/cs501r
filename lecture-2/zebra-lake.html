Zebra in the Lake

The zebra starts at the center of a circular lake and swims at speed v_zebra. The lion is confined to the shore and moves along it at speed v_lion, always turning to chase the zebra's current bearing. The lion can only catch the zebra the instant it reaches the shore. Draw a path for the zebra, control it live, or watch the theoretically optimal escape strategy.
